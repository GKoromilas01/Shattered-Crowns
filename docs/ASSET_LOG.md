## Leonardo.Ai licensing reply (received 2026-11-17, from Leonardo support "Marck", Level 2)
Summary of what support wrote (their own caveat: they explain the Terms, they do not give legal advice for our project; independent legal advice is recommended if images are central to a commercial product):
- Free plan images may be used commercially, including in a game we sell (Terms of Service clause 8.7). Ownership of free-plan outputs vests in Leonardo.Ai; we receive a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence including commercial use. No attribution required.
- Free-plan images are Public Content: other users can view, download and remix them. Rights are not exclusive.
- Status is fixed at generation time. Upgrading later only affects new images. Existing free images stay Public Content under free-plan terms. Hiding/removing them later does not undo rights already granted.
- Private Mode applies to new generations only. For assets we want to own and keep private, generate on a paid plan with Private Mode on, before finalising.
- No restriction by model or style; Lucid Origin is covered. Game art, character portraits and UI backgrounds are permitted uses.
- Watermark-like text in outputs is a known artefact and does not affect rights, but do not use any output that resembles a real brand or trademark.
- References: https://leonardo.ai/terms-of-service (Section 8), https://intercom.help/leonardo-ai/en/articles/8044018-commercial-usage, https://intercom.help/leonardo-ai/en/articles/8044029-private-public-images

Policy for this project: key assets that define the game's identity (main portraits, logo, cut scenes) should be generated on a paid plan with Private Mode on, or made with a generator whose terms give us ownership. Free-plan images are acceptable for non-defining art. Check every output for brand-like text. Record the generator, plan, privacy mode and date for every image in this log.
