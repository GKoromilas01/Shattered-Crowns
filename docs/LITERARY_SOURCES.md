# Words for the road — sources

Inspected 11 November 2026. All quoted text is English from authors whose works are in the public domain; no modern translation or recording is used. The settlement sayings are original game writing and labelled that way in the UI. They are not historical proverbs.

## William Shakespeare, Sonnets (1609)

Project Gutenberg ebook 1041, *Shakespeare's Sonnets*: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1041

Inspected text mirror: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/GITenberg/Shakespeare-s-Sonnets_1041/master/1041.txt

- Sonnet XVIII: “Rough winds do shake the darling buds of May,”
- Sonnet LX: “Like as the waves make towards the pebbled shore, / So do our minutes hasten to their end;”

These are excerpts, not complete poems. Numbering is shown in Arabic numerals in the game. Original punctuation is retained.

## William Blake, On Another's Sorrow (1789)

From *Songs of Innocence*, included in Project Gutenberg ebook 574, *Poems of William Blake*: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/574

Inspected text mirror: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/GITenberg/Poems-of-William-Blake_574/master/574.txt

Excerpt: “Can I see another's grief, / And not seek for kind relief?”

The interlude is optional original presentation of the player's existing settlement. It makes no claim to depict Shakespeare's or Blake's world or a historical event. No poem recording, music, illustration, or other third-party asset is imported.
