# Early shipyards and river landings — owner reference, 22 November 2026

Owner supplied three film stills as mood/material references, explicitly requesting something similar rather than a copy. These direct future original shipyard/boat artwork; they are not implementation assets or permission to add a naval system to the current release.

## First stage: a village landing

- Small open wooden rowing boats, visible ribs/benches and practical oars; modest passenger and light-cargo capacity.
- Boats drawn close to the bank or a low timber landing, with mooring rope and posts. A natural dirt/stone path reaches the water.
- A rough timber boat shed/work area, simple repair stocks, poles, fishing nets and ropes. Barrels, woven baskets, crates and sacks are used sparingly to suggest real loading and village work.
- Weathered timber, worn stone, earth and calm water. An irregular natural shore, not a polished metropolitan waterfront.
- Workers carry or load goods according to actual orders when that gameplay exists; decoration must not imply simulated cargo that does not exist.

## Progression

Start small and legible at normal game zoom. Larger vessels, longer jetties, cranes, stone quays and busy harbours belong to later earned development. Do not make the first structure resemble a fortress or royal naval base.
