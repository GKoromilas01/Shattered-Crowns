# Milestone 22 — A Living Settlement

Based on the owner's desktop playtest of released v0.21. Owner delegated routine decisions and asked for continued graphics, sound, cutscene and literary improvements. Mobile expansion remains paused. This candidate does not authorize a merge or publication.

## Fewer repeated orders

After finishing a civic project, its builders automatically travel to the nearest unfinished project with fewer than four assigned builders. Travelling builders count toward capacity; equal-distance projects use stable worksite order. Manual orders, release and cancellation take precedence. When no eligible project remains, builders become idle. Existing construction speeds, material costs, cancellation refunds and saved build orders remain unchanged. This is city construction automation, not a change to skirmish.

## A clearer screen

The large duplicate building-information box drawn over the map is removed. Selection rings remain, and the existing screen-space inspector keeps the details and workplace actions. Worksites and the inspector replace each other rather than stacking. Selection changes and Escape clear stale placement notices; completing placement clears the old Theater instruction. Browser context menus are suppressed on the game surface while right-click orders and drag panning remain. Resource selection accepts a modest additional near-miss margin and chooses the nearest deposit within it.

## A little more atmosphere

Buildings gain cached soft contact shading and warmer house-roof highlights. The original Canvas art and renderer remain. A restrained synthesized completion chord uses the existing effects channel, master volume, mute and hidden-tab handling. Simultaneous completions coalesce to avoid repetitive sound.

In **Chronicle → Words for the road**, **Watch settlement interlude** frames the current map for twelve seconds with three short captions. Return to city, Escape or keyboard activation ends it immediately. Simulation is suspended, gameplay shortcuts are blocked, and the original map view and pause state are preserved. Reduced-motion preferences remove the gentle camera movement. Changing city, restoring a save or hiding the tab cleans up the scene. No automatic interruptions or new permanent HUD messages are introduced.

**Poetry & settlement sayings** is optional reading. Excerpts from Shakespeare and Blake show their author and work, link to the source edition, and are distinguished from original game sayings. See [literary sources](LITERARY_SOURCES.md). No modern translation, recording, external art, paid service or asset purchase is included.

The scene is a first cinematic presentation of the existing world, not a new opening chapter, quest chain, dialogue system or video production. Those can build on this foundation after owner review.
