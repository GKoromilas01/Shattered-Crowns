# v0.11.0 — Village Steward

Open **City Life → Village** in either playable home, or in classic mode. The board shows recruited map workers separately from civilian workplace staffing, current gold/wood/stone and remaining deposits across the city map.

**Select idle workers** selects only living friendly workers without orders, targets or carried resources. Busy workers keep their orders. **Locate wood/stone/gold** finds the active deposit nearest your Keep without issuing orders or changing selection. Select workers, locate a deposit, then right-click it to gather using the existing controls. Depleted sites recover after45simulationseconds; Pause freezes that recovery. Disabled actions explain availability through the displayed counts.

Village needs link to existing Food, Housing and Employment details. They use last measured food cycles and current housing/staffing/service data. Service gaps include vacant built homes, and are preparation guidance rather than proof that every service is required for migration. Remaining map resources are separate from stock already gathered. This is guidance, not automatic worker assignment or a promise of balanced long-term growth.

No simulation balance, employment order, building cost, road behavior, audio or save-format change. Schemas1/2/3 remain; a genuine Brooksteadv0.10save is included in compatibility regressions. Existing controls, peaceful mode, world navigation and all prior save fixtures remain covered.

This is a draft dependent on PR15, not an approved merge or production release. No backend, paid service or infrastructure is introduced.
