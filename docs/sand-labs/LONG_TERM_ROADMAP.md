# Shattered Crowns — Long-Term Vision and Feature Register
**Status:** Owner ideas captured 2026-11-05. **Future proposals, not implementation approval.** No feature below is currently promised or shipped by this document. Protect existing city-building-first design and merge/deploy gates.

## Core principle
A believable fantasy civilization simulation: economic prosperity, service quality, public trust, political legitimacy and diplomacy matter as much as military power. War is expensive, destabilizing and rare enough to be consequential.

## Conquest, occupation and political consequences
A victorious player may preserve a conquered settlement and its workers/buildings, partially dismantle it, or raze it. Every option has meaningful consequences for production, displaced people, rebuilding cost, legitimacy, loyalty, rebellion risk, diplomatic reputation, and long-term prosperity. Prevent griefing/harassment and protect new players; design fair PvP, opt-in safeguards and recovery mechanics before implementation. Do not make destruction a cost-free optimal strategy.

## World economy and currency
- One **fictional, non-redeemable game coin** initially, used to price/exchange goods and services in player and NPC markets; retain distinct physical resources such as wood, stone, gold, food and manufactured goods.
- Continental trade hubs and a global exchange with transparent supply/demand, transport cost, scarcity, fees, historical prices, anti-manipulation rules and market information. Stock-exchange-like UI is a design metaphor, not real securities trading.
- Economic model should connect employment, wages, tax, production, storage, transport, food security, inflation/deflation, public services and political satisfaction. Tune for engaging gameplay, not exact real-world complexity.
- **Potential future blockchain/cryptocurrency:** separate legal/technical/commercial feasibility study; no token launch, promise of cash value, redeemability or player earnings without owner decision and specialist regulatory review.

## Governance, councils and messaging
- Player-requested councils on economy, war, taxes, diplomacy and crises, subject to quorum/consent and rules.
- World events bulletin: wars, shortages, disasters, market stress, treaties and historic milestones; configurable scope, rate limits and notification preferences.
- Admin messages for updates, downtime and player safety; player-to-player messages with blocking, moderation, anti-spam and reporting.
- Forums, help desk, searchable manual, onboarding and player support policies.

## Living history
Persistent in-game **Chronicles of Shattered Crowns**: eras, rulers, settlement growth, wars, councils, landmarks, trade booms and declines. Preserve provenance and distinguish public history from private information. Historical entries should be readable by era, kingdom, city and player campaign.

## Buildings, landmarks and RPG-lite progression
- Distinctive legendary landmarks for cities/kingdoms; original names, lore, art and balanced bonuses.
- Upgradable housing, production and civic buildings with visible tiers and meaningful tradeoffs.
- Workshops for armour, weapons and advanced equipment; equipment quality depends on production chains, materials and building tier.
- Stables, cavalry, expanded unit types, recruitment/training, veteran progression and limited RPG-lite specializations; avoid turning peaceful city-building into constant combat.
- Deep but simple UI: show basic actions at top level; move advanced operations into the relevant buildings (e.g., smithy upgrades equipment, barracks trains units, market manages orders, council hall hosts councils).

## Business model and player rewards — REQUIRES FEASIBILITY REVIEW
- Launch free to build and observe a genuine player community.
- Evaluate Steam premium/early-access, optional subscription, cosmetics and other ethical monetization later; avoid pay-to-win.
- Owner idea: return **20% of subscription receipts** to top-performing players. This is **not approved as a program**. First define gross vs net, eligibility, scoring, taxes, fraud, anti-botting, geography, age, contest/gambling classification, consumer rules and sustainability. Consider non-cash recognition or prizes initially.
- Owner idea: compensated playtesting for 1–2 days at **€10 for eight hours**. This is an idea, **not a lawful pay rate determination**. Before hiring, check applicable employment/contractor rules, Greek minimum wages, insurance, taxation, consent and safeguarding. Prefer small, fairly compensated, scoped tests and recorded feedback.
- No paid advertising, subscription, platform registration, cryptocurrency integration or hiring without explicit owner authorization.

## Growth and promotion research
Prioritize a playable demo, Steam wishlist funnel, developer updates, community/creator outreach, short original gameplay clips, organic YouTube/X, then small measured paid tests. Research Google Ads, YouTube Ads, X Ads and creators; measure cost per relevant visit, wishlist, engaged tester and retained player. Never assume advertising spend guarantees traction.

### Initial budget hypotheses (not vendor quotes)
- Organic/community prelaunch: €0 media spend, but substantial production/moderation time.
- Small paid validation: €300–€600 total across 2–4 weeks, contingent on an effective landing page and analytics.
- Wider early-access promotion: €1,500–€3,000+ only after validating conversion and retention.
Platform prices are auction-based and vary. ## Suggested sequencing (not fixed milestones)
1. **Now:** Milestone 18 governor starts, geography, illustrated settlements; fix confirmed usability issues only.
2. **Next:** building upgrades, smithy, stables, deeper building-local menus, unit progression, landmark concepts; protect core economy.
3. **Then:** simulation-balanced trade hubs, fictional coin, economic dashboards, news feed and chronicles.
4. **Later:** multiplayer architecture, councils, diplomacy, occupation/conquest, moderation, support systems.
5. **Separate commercial/legal track:** Steam, subscriptions, paid testers, reward scheme and possible tokenization only after evidence and specialist review.

## Future design tests
- Peaceful play remains viable and competitive; military aggression has substantial direct and indirect costs.
- Occupation decisions affect satisfaction, labour, output, reputation and stability, with no dominant exploit.
- No unlimited coin creation, circular arbitrage, bot-driven rewards or pay-to-win.
- Markets respond predictably to supply/demand and transport; explain prices clearly to players.
- Deep menus remain discoverable, keyboard-accessible and usable on phones.
- Every major feature has explicit save/migration, accessibility, performance, balance, security and moderation plans.

## Open decisions
Define victory and success without rewarding pure aggression; decide opt-in PvP rules, how historical records persist across games, what fraction of the economy is player-driven, and whether financial reward concepts should remain entirely outside gameplay. The owner will decide monetization only after a feasibility review.
