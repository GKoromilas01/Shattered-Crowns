# Future world direction

Owner decisions, 11 November 2026. These guide later proposals; they are not implemented systems, a delivery schedule or permission to spend/publish.

- Keep the main world grounded in people, economics, trade, politics and warfare. Religion, magicians and other supernatural characters can exist with limited, explainable effects such as modest healing or strength; the world must not revolve around magic.
- Improve graphics gradually with each version. The founder's harbour reference shows dense warm-stone waterfront architecture, deep blue water, ships, cargo and busy quays. Use this as an original visual target; prototype one playable harbour before committing to an engine migration or cinematic-scale production.
- Keep free civic placement and useful roads. Measure road speed benefits before adding them; do not silently replace existing logistics with CaesarIII-style walking-service dependencies. Let new towns grow more readable while preserving crowded legacy saves.
- The current city is a finite2450×900 local map; the atlas is a separate overview. Owner confirmed full-map visibility explained the apparent scrolling problem. Long-term goal: continue travelling through countryside to neighbouring cities, then across connected world regions. Start with one caravan route and a meaningful neighbour interaction; plan persistent travel and regional loading before enlarging the campaign.
- Multiplayer progress should be authoritative on a server. Resume means rejoin the current shared state, not restore an earlier personal copy. First explore private cooperative sessions with explicit host pause/resume and reconnection rules; public persistent or competitive worlds require offline/disconnection rules. Retain local saves for single-player. No multiplayer service, account or hosting cost is activated now.
- Regional architecture, names, clothing, resources, faiths, art and music should draw from researched cultures, not racial stereotypes or an equation of continents with poverty/slavery. Historical learning entries need verified attribution and rights-aware translations. Mixed-era inspirations belong to an explicitly original setting.
- Preserve the wider backlog: one coherent opening chapter; regional goods/caravans/manufacturing; diplomacy, officers and consequential court events; taxes/corruption/jury; education/medicine/animal farming; seasonal/environmental effects; navies/pirates and optional bounded powers. Add systems in small playable slices.
- Owner requested 13 November 2026: in a later version, add a complete, easy-to-find **keyboard shortcuts** reference inside Help & Guide. Cover all/idle worker selection (O all idle, I next idle, W all workers), gathering, construction, units, camera, panels and Pause; audit actual bindings and context restrictions before publishing. Keep the reference synchronized with changes and usable by keyboard. This is deferred from the v0.24 shipment scope.
- Compare earned powers and fair counterplay before considering any paid abilities. Payments, ads, subscriptions, microtransactions, token ideas, fundraising and charitable announcements stay inactive until explicit later instructions. Marketing tools/prices/budgets need dated research and a defined financial basis.
- External articles/videos are inspiration and research leads, not automatic permission to copy images/music/code. Inspect actual source material, record application and licence, or create original work. Do not invent summaries of inaccessible videos. Detailed historical/competitor/AI-tool research remains a separate backlog.
