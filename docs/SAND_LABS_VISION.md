# Sand Labs and Shattered Crowns: founder's long-term ideas

Recorded 2 November 2026. These are Giannis's ideas for staged exploration, not release promises or authorization to spend money, issue tokens, collect player data, or make charitable claims.

## Brand

Company name remains **Sand Labs**; “Sound Labs”, “sun lamps”, and “Saturn crowns” have appeared in voice transcription. Game name remains **Shattered Crowns**.

Giannis likes the current Sand Labs logo, but wants its future identity to evoke the **Oracle of Delphi**: mystery, discovery, wisdom, and curiosity about what lies behind the symbol. Preserve the current approved direction while gathering more founder input. Explore an original abstract oracle/light/threshold motif; avoid copying an existing logo or presenting the company as predicting financial outcomes.

## Visual quality

Graphics are a product priority alongside mechanics and AI. An original [city art-direction illustration](visual-concepts/city-art-direction.png) has been delivered as a concept, not a renderer upgrade. Before expanding the whole game's art, create one representative playable city scene with a coherent palette, readable building silhouettes, terrain, animation, UI typography, and restrained effects. Assess it at actual play scale on desktop and mobile. Compare the improved scene with the current game, then apply the chosen art direction gradually. Original or licensed assets only; retain clear ownership and attribution records. Founder feedback should concern appeal and readability rather than debugging individual controls.

## Smarter game AI

Start with bounded, inspectable game systems: advisors that explain actual city conditions, opponents that plan under resource constraints, and personalities with distinct strategies. Separate peaceful city play from future military encounters.

For adaptation, propose opt-in aggregate playtest metrics, explicit difficulty bounds, and versioned balancing experiments. Evaluate candidate changes against fixed scenario benchmarks and human playtests before release, with rollback available. Opponents can adapt within a session without rewriting production code or silently changing player saves. A hosted language model for optional narrative/advisor dialogue is a later decision requiring cost, latency, privacy, and moderation design; core simulation should work without it. No promise of unsupervised self-improvement.

## Sand Coin: research only

Giannis suggested a possible **Sand Coin**, with its relationship to EUR/USD to be explored. No issuance, peg, exchange, investment product, trading, fundraising, or payment integration is approved. First clarify whether the need is simply nontransferable in-game currency. A normal game currency can meet that need without a blockchain. If a transferable token is later considered, commission jurisdiction-specific legal, tax, security, and economic review before making commitments. Do not make game quality or launch dependent on a token.

## Social contribution

Giannis wants to consider a small contribution from the business toward poverty, cancer and other illnesses, children, or education. Initial proposal: once operating finances support it, choose a vetted registered education/children's charity and donate a clearly defined fraction of company receipts after taxes, refunds, and platform fees (or a fixed affordable company budget). Agree the exact basis, timing, cap/minimum, recipient, and accounting with the founder before announcement. Publish periodic totals and evidence of completed transfers; distinguish promised from paid amounts. No player surcharge, charity partnership claim, or donation collection is authorized now.

## Suggested sequence

1. Finish and verify the current city/kingdom milestone, audio, start screen, and placement feedback.
2. Obtain founder approval before merging or deploying the milestone.
3. Develop a representative visual-quality scene and obtain art-direction feedback.
4. Add bounded AI planning with measurable scenario tests.
5. Revisit commercial model and an affordable, transparent social-contribution policy.
6. Revisit brand mystery/oracle exploration and Sand Coin research as separate decisions.

Founder can add inspiration and constraints over time. These ideas should remain in the roadmap until explicitly accepted, revised, or declined.
