# Proposed v0.28 — Beyond the Town Gates

Owner direction: the next release should be a substantial gameplay improvement. Current opening/UI fixes and diplomacy access amend v0.27; they do not justify a separate small release. This is a proposal, not implemented work or approval to merge/deploy.

## One connected chapter

Start in any peaceful town, send an envoy to explore, discover established neighbours, and make a diplomatic choice that changes actual trade and your town's opportunities.

- **Visible exploration:** regional destinations and routes reveal through paid missions. Discovery persists in saves; mission markers reflect actual progress. Keep existing city camera, movement and resource access. No seamless travel or simulated physical caravans is implied.
- **Several meaningful partners:** retain Ironmere and add two contacts from the established world catalogue, after checking their existing lore. Give each an original portrait, different needs and distinct offers. Contacts are reached through play, without requiring a special starting kingdom.
- **Lasting consequences:** a compact relationship record responds to fulfilling, declining or breaking clearly stated commitments. Negotiations consume real goods. Their results change actual contract terms, available batches or reciprocal aid, with reasons visible before committing.
- **A complete outcome:** a two- or three-stage chapter ends with a visible trading relationship and a factual Chronicle entry. Advisor explains the player's current resource/workforce tradeoff and the next useful action.

## Delivery target

The release should let the player discover something, meet more than one neighbour, choose between materially different outcomes and see those outcomes affect the settlement. Original map/portrait artwork and readable mission feedback are part of delivery. No extra floating HUD panels or disconnected menu collection.

First provide a short playable prototype of the complete discovery → negotiation → real consequence loop for owner review, then expand and validate it. Preserve old saves, prices/terms already committed to shipments, sound/reduced-motion preferences and native keyboard access. Full CI/package gates remain, with CI finishing independently rather than long waits for each small fix.

Classic Skirmish remains combat-focused. Diplomacy there should follow a separate design for ceasefires, alliances and AI behaviour. Full warfare expansion, multiplayer and mobile expansion remain outside this proposal.

Exact contacts, mission costs, relationship thresholds, contract terms and release scope still need a reviewed design before implementation. This proposal is the recommended direction for that design.
