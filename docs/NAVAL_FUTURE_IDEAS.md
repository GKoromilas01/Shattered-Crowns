# Naval progression ideas — owner discussion,22 November2026

Future proposals, not implemented in v059:

- Crew experience earned through completed voyages/patrols: better fishing and trading efficiency, steadier combat. Recommended first upgrade because individual ships develop a history worth preserving.
- Hull repairs and paid reinforcement at the home Shipyard. Consider repair cost against replacement cost rather than automatic free healing.
- Nets and fishing equipment: bigger catch or shorter work time, balanced against boat cost and fish-ground capacity.
- Merchant cargo holds: larger committed cargo and payment; display exact costs and reserve resources only once.
- Veteran crews, ship names and promotion choices later. Transporting people/troops is a separate cargo-ship feature; the owner currently confirmed the existing merchant workflow.

Buildings retain construction; ship-specific upgrades belong on each selected ship's panel. Keep diplomacy initially simple, reusing established contact/trust systems. Alliances, marriage and wider overseas warfare are later systems.
