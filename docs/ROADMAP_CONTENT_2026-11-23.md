# Content roadmap — owner ideas reviewed and agreed (23 November 2026)

The owner supplied a long list of story, scene and simulation ideas. Claude reviewed them, the owner agreed with every recommendation below. Nothing here is built yet. Each version bundles a few of these, each checkable in about 30 seconds; Claude sets the order.

## Rules that apply to all of it
- Cost/benefit balance, no over-information: one simple "quality" level per building, extra menus only if the player opens them.
- Every new graphic or video must look like the current game style, or be a dramatic, all-round upgrade (then the owner may choose to replace graphics widely later). Never mix styles.
- Cards become graphics or videos gradually (decision scenes first).
- Pace and costs are tuned later, once the content exists. Current pace stays.
- Voices and coins/languages per continent come after the scene system exists.

## Strong, fit the game (build first)
1. Yearly rhythm: city anniversary parade; two chapel holidays; one castle feast.
2. Throne decision scenes (jury for traitors, citizen disputes, messenger news). Replaces cards one by one.
3. Click a peasant, worker or soldier: a short line in the continent's own language, no translation on the map.
4. Visual identity per continent through palette, roofs and building styling, later language and coins.
5. Education and experience improve armory, shipyard, farms, lumberyards, hospitals: one quality level per building.
6. Village-to-village raids that damage relations (not total war).

## Good, later, in small pieces
- Spies, double agents, assassination plots, king's personal guard (one plot type at a time).
- Medals (copper/silver/gold stars) and admirals once combat is stable.
- Funerals, families swearing revenge (video each, after the scene system).
- Foreign-language voiceovers with English subtitles in cutscenes.

## Careful or cut (owner agreed)
- Northern and island peoples are fierce and primitive through ritual fires, masks, war paint; no graphic sexual or gore scenes.
- Do not use skin colour as the main faction marker. Use banners, armour colours, architecture, names and languages. Allies share similar colours with slight variation, rivals clearly different; same-continent fights use home/away colours.
- Do not build 25 historical scenarios or a 12-city council at once. Pick about three campaigns (candidates: Byzantium with Justinian/Theodora/Hagia Sophia, Vikings, the Crusades); the rest is flavour.

## Open questions
- Hyperframes (AI video tool): only if a test clip matches our current style exactly or is dramatically better. Test one clip before any plan.
- Owner uploaded city pictures per continent; they were not accessible in the session. Ask him to attach them again.
- YouTube subtitle files: dropped for now by the owner.
- Anthems: more music later when credits allow; original one-minute pieces stay out of the game until upgraded.

## Decided later on 23 November 2026
- Opening: existing clips slowed to 12–20 s with the subtitles shown over the video and describing what is on screen (owner liked the sample). Style of the lines: Shakespeare. Text is in OPENING_SUBTITLES_SHAKESPEARE.md. Planned for v0.62.

## Standing style rule (owner, 23 November 2026)
All new narrative text from now on is written in a Shakespearean manner (blank verse or lightly archaic English): subtitles, voiceover scripts, cutscene and decision-scene lines, messenger news, story cards turned into scenes, villager lines in English. Original writing only, never quotations from the plays.
- Interpretation to confirm with the owner: functional interface text (buttons, tooltips, costs, Tester steps, error messages) stays plain so the game remains easy to use.
- Foreign-language villager and soldier lines per continent keep their own language with no translation; the Shakespearean style applies to the English-speaking ones and to every English subtitle.
- Every new clip (school, chapel, theater and later ones) gets Shakespearean subtitles that describe what is on screen.
