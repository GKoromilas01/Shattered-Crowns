# First Winter — approved direction, v0.68 candidate

Owner approved the settlement-first proposal on 28 November: reuse current gathering, construction, upgrades, resources, defenses and saves; add a complete guided scenario before broader armies/diplomacy. Current main is v0.66; a separate v0.67 fixes branch exists and must not be overwritten. This candidate branches from main and is v0.68.

Approach: an optional First Winter charter at the Keep, using the existing city simulation, versus a separate duplicate city engine or a broad simulation rewrite. Choose the integrated charter: preserves the existing painted game and lets its real economy determine success. No paid assets, new buildings or music are required because the proposed building roles already exist. Desktop first. No automatic release/self-merge.

Start only by explicit action at the Keep. Duration 480 simulation seconds, approximately24 active real minutes at existing one-third speed. Pause, overview and background do not advance the simulation. Pending decisions remain available until answered; unresolved choices block the next event/final assessment without stopping normal city play. At120/240/360 seconds offer a food caravan (40gold→30food or decline), cold snap (30wood→8happiness or -8happiness), and stone merchant (25gold→30stone or decline). Choices transact once, revalidate costs and survive manual/autosave/export/restore. No change to global resource rates or army AI. Winter is an optional readiness challenge, not a claim of a new weather renderer.

At480seconds assess six simultaneous checks: two living houses; a staffed/unpaused Farm or Fishery; food>=max(40,2*population) and production>=demand; happiness>=55; wood>=40 and stone>=20; two living soldiers/archers or a living Watchtower. Failure leaves an improving/review state: player can fix deficits and explicitly reassess. Success records completion without ending sandbox play or paying repeat rewards. Keep panel presents countdown, readiness, event choices, restart-free outcome and links to existing employment/gathering controls. Existing guide Tester gains manual instructions.

Save schema18 is used only when a charter exists. Ensure native naval/trade prerequisites, allow null war for schema18, keep all1–17 saves unchanged when charter absent. Strict bounded charter fields validated before city replacement. Older clients cannot import18; recommend export backup. Existing art/music/media bundles retained byte-for-byte. Publish candidate PR for owner/Claude approval; do not update hub to an unapproved candidate.
