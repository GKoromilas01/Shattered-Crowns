# v0.24 — Ironmere's first shipment

Owner approved the timed shipment proposal and refinements on 13 November 2026, then instructed implementation. Base: released main 77690e89b3417730eb648400fcffd2b8caa70410. Feature branch/PR authorized; no merge or deployment without separate approval.

## One playable decision
An eligible Marches city receives Ironmere's first trade request. Existing neighbour Ironmere and Lady Elowen retain their identities; the timber request is new original proposed story content approved for this chapter. Commit 40 actual wood instead of spending it locally; receive 36 stone after 60 outbound and 60 returning game seconds. One shipment per city, no repeat farming, escort units, random losses, reputation currency or simulated neighbour economy. Existing basket exports/sales, prices, growth, staffing and gathering remain unchanged.

Approaches considered: a timed shipment through existing controls, or a visible travelling unit requiring new movement/map-boundary/selection/save behavior. Owner selected the timed shipment. No claim of seamless regional travel or a physical caravan unit. Mobile expansion remains paused.

## Eligibility and progression
Peaceful city mode; origin home or brookstead in the Marches; living Keep; an occupied watered/road-serviced house with nobody displaced; measured food production covering demand without shortfall and stock covering four current demand cycles; an open road-connected market staffed to at least 50 percent. No chapter requirement for sandbox. A completed Brookstead charter receives appropriate follow-up wording; either charter choice has identical terms.

Offer creation occurs on a real economy cycle, never snapshot/UI reads. Offer may be inspected while prerequisites later lapse; Send rechecks live eligibility and at least 40 wood. Viewing, Later and abandoning preparation cost nothing. Send deducts wood once and escrows it. No recall after departure; this is explicit before dispatch. At 60 game seconds phase changes to returning; at 120 the shipment credits 36 stone once, consumes escrow, marks fulfilled and records an immutable factual Chronicle outcome. Market demolition or later food loss does not strand a departed shipment. The existing city clock controls travel; Pause, atlas freeze and closed browser grant no progress. New city/reset discards the old city's shipment; no cross-city refunds or rewards.

## Offer, Market and City Life
A small non-pausing dismissible request appears once when the offer is ready and no title, atlas, interlude, menu or competing dialog is active. View offer opens trade details; Later leaves a persistent Trade offer available indicator. Selecting an owned completed Market exposes Trade offers. City Life also exposes the same details/state. No construction-menu button is mistaken for an operational Market. Both entrances share one transaction controller. Native keyboard activation, focus return, Escape and stale held input after restore must remain safe. No automatic dispatch, deductions or camera motion.

## Sayings
Short original sayings appear when a new peaceful city begins, the First Charter completes, and the first shipment returns. Each milestone is queued once in city state, displayed for seven visible wall-clock seconds then faded out; reduced motion removes the fade. They never pause, take focus, intercept controls or require a click. Trade requests/active dialogs take priority: a saying waits or temporarily hides with its remaining visible time retained. A saved displayed saying never replays on restore; queued unseen sayings remain pending. Chronicle History retains all earned sayings with original attribution and milestone labels. Older saves gain no invented start/chapter history.

## Persistence
Schema 7 stores nullable chapter state, regional state, construction and build-order references. Newly created peaceful cities use schema 7 to persist initial/milestone notices honestly; skirmish and legacy schema 1–6 round trips retain their previous rules unless a new regional event actually occurs. Loading older saves does not silently enroll a chapter or fabricate past sayings. Strictly validate enums, exact terms, times, phase/progress/escrow/outcome relationships, origins, unique milestones and bounds before replacing a city. Restore paused, without offline progress. A historical fulfilled agreement survives market demolition. Earlier clients cannot read schema 7; portable older checkpoints remain separate. Duplicate clicks/reads, repeated restores and two UI entrances cannot create duplicate cargo or rewards. Restoring a genuinely earlier single-player checkpoint restores its earlier resources and history together, not a global reward ledger.
