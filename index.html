🌾 HOW YOUR CITY WORKS
🌾 Farms: food is produced automatically every 5 seconds when citizens staff the farm. No manual harvesting.
🏚 Granaries: store up to +160 food each; food is shared automatically, no delivery commands.
🛒 Markets: staffed markets improve tax income and happiness. Build at least 3 connected roads and place the market within 135 pixels of that network for a tax bonus.
🛣 Roads: town council builds them without selecting a worker. Connected groups of at least 3 roads can boost nearby markets; roads do not yet affect troop movement.
🛡 Towers / Fortresses / Walls: require a selected worker within 180 pixels; a blue circle shows that construction range. All other buildings need no selected worker.
🏘 Houses: increase population capacity; more people means more employees and food consumption.

❔ Help — Shattered Crowns

🌾 How Your City Works
🏘 Getting Started & Buildings

Build houses for population capacity, farms for food, wells for housing happiness and granaries for food storage. Use Quick Build to place structures. Check building costs by hovering over buttons.

Road networks support market bonuses. Coastal fisheries provide food. Workers gather wood, gold and stone, while citizens staff production and service buildings.

🖱 Mouse & Keyboard Controls

Left-click: select a unit. Left-drag: select a group with a rectangle. Right-click: move or attack. Screen edges: scroll the map. Middle-drag: pan the map. Double-click: select visible units of the same type.

I: next idle worker; O: all idle workers; K: all workers; N: next idle soldier; U: all idle troops; Y: archers; T: soldiers; M: army; Z: visible army; W: wounded; L: healthy troops. Ctrl+1/2/3: save groups; 1/2/3: recall groups.

⚔ Army & Combat

Train soldiers and archers from Army & Workers. Use right-click to command selected units. Tactical actions include defend keep (D), retreat (R), hold position (G), shield wall (V), charge (C), protect archers (B), evacuate wounded (J) and Field Medic (H).

🏛 Kingdoms & Politics

Use Maps to explore the fantasy realms and settlements. Open Politics to inspect governance, kingdom affairs and treasury simulations. Multiplayer elections and real-money transactions are not live in this prototype.

Press F1 to open or close Help. The City Advisor remains available for interactive tests.
